AnKing Images stores saved_images.csv in this folder.

Anki preserves the user_files folder when the add-on is upgraded. You may back
up or inspect the CSV while Anki is closed. Do not rename its columns. The add-on
stores only the selected image filenames/sources in its suspended AnKing Images
catalogue card for sync; the richer CSV metadata remains local. Reviewer stars
change only the CSV. Use the Sync button in My Images to pool all image names
from the CSV and Anki card, then save that combined list to both.
