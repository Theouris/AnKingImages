AnKing Images stores saved_images.csv in this folder.

Anki preserves the user_files folder when the add-on is upgraded. You may back
up or inspect the CSV while Anki is closed. Do not rename its columns. The add-on
stores the selected image IDs and subcategories in its suspended ~AnKing Images
catalogue card for sync; the richer CSV metadata remains local. Reviewer
bookmarks change only the CSV. Use the Sync button in My Images to pool all
image/subcategory pairs from the CSV and Anki card, then save that combined list
to both. For an image found in both places, the CSV subcategory takes priority.
